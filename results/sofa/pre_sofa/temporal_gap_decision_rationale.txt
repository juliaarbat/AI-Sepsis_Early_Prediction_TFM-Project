Temporal-gap exclusion rationale

Rule used: exclude episodes with maximum temporal gap >30 days only when the episode has no evidence of a critical-care stay.

Reasoning: a very large gap without a critical-care record is treated as an unexplained interruption of the daily episode timeline. A large gap with a critical-care record is kept because the missing days may be clinically explained by the critical-care stay and should not be removed automatically.
